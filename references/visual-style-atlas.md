# Visual Style Atlas

Use this reference to select a coherent aesthetic system beyond Chinese fantasy and giant spectacle. Choose one primary `aesthetic_family`; use at most one `secondary_influence`, and state its `mix_rule` as a division of labor.

## Aesthetic Families

| Family | Visible mechanism | Strong uses | Protect against |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cinematic_naturalism` | motivated light, human-scale lensing, ordinary surfaces, partial information | drama, history, documentary-like fiction | advertising polish and decorative rim light |
| `noir_chiaroscuro` | black fields, shaped hard light, partial concealment, reflective accents | crime, secrecy, moral pressure, luxury darkness | generic blue-purple cyberpunk |
| `heightened_expressionism` | one controlled distortion in geometry, shadow, perspective, or color | horror, psychological pressure, operatic action | stacking every dramatic device |
| `surreal_oneiric` | stable motif plus impossible relation, clean dream causality | memory, dream, paradox, conceptual campaigns | random surreal objects |
| `romantic_sublime` | small human presence against weather, terrain, cosmic or sacred scale | awe, danger, pilgrimage, nature, myth | “epic” without scale evidence |
| `graphic_modernism` | grid, hierarchy, limited shapes, disciplined typography and negative space | posters, identities, infographics, campaigns | decorative clutter and fake sophistication |
| `retro_analog` | medium-owned grain, halation, print/film response, period color and wear | memory, music, archival mood, tactile campaigns | generic dirt and fake age |
| `luxury_editorial` | restraint, one hero, sculptural spacing, material precision, controlled polish | fashion, product, beauty, architecture | gold/gloss everywhere |
| `tactile_handcrafted` | visible paper, clay, felt, wood, stitching, tool marks, physical seams | stop motion, children/family, craft brands, poetic worlds | plastic CG or classroom-craft drift |
| `painterly_illustrative` | medium-specific marks, value grouping, edge hierarchy, selective detail | concept art, editorial illustration, historical or fantasy work | uniform brush noise and over-rendering |
| `animation_stylized` | simplified forms, silhouette rules, character/background separation, controlled line/shape | 2D, 3D NPR, comics, family, game concepts | accidental live-action conversion |
| `documentary_observational` | available light, found geometry, honest texture, event over pose | social observation, reportage, real processes | staged perfection and beauty grading |
| `speculative_worldbuilding` | social/technology/material rules expressed through locations and objects | science fiction, alternative history, ecological futures | generic neon panels and unrelated lore |
| `minimal_object_study` | reduction, material presence, negative space, precise contact and shadow | products, food, scientific objects, quiet concepts | emptiness without tension |
| `archival_historical` | source hierarchy, period reproduction behavior, paper/print/plate evidence | history, museums, memory, documentary composites | false certainty and mixed periods |
| `mixed_media` | explicit layer ownership and interaction between two compatible media | collage, photo-illustration, 2D/3D, projection, experimental work | style salad and unowned effects |

## Capture or Render Methods

The method describes how the image appears to have been made. It does not claim the model actually used that production process.

- `photography`: still-camera lens, exposure, sensor/film response, real surface truth.
- `live_action_cinema`: staging, production design, motivated lighting, film-camera behavior.
- `photoreal_cg`: physically based materials, accurate light transport, camera/lens realism.
- `stylized_3d`: simplified form with coherent physical light and controlled NPR rules.
- `2d_animation`: line/shape economy, character/background separation, compositing logic.
- `illustration`: authored line, shape, value, edge, and mark behavior.
- `ink_wash`: wet/dry boundaries, brush pressure, absorbent paper, negative space.
- `watercolor_gouache`: pigment bloom or opaque shape, paper tooth, controlled edge loss.
- `oil_paint`: layered opacity, brush direction, impasto or glazing, canvas behavior.
- `printmaking`: carved or screened shapes, limited inks, paper grain, registration behavior.
- `collage_photomontage`: visible source seams, scale contrast, torn/cut edges, deliberate assembly.
- `stop_motion`: physical puppet/object tactility, frame-by-frame pose logic, miniature lighting.
- `miniature_practical`: believable scale model, real light falloff, atmospheric and depth cues that avoid toy-like accident.
- `paper_craft`: layered paper planes, fiber, folds, cuts, and cast shadows.
- `archival_composite`: photographs, documents, plates, maps, or scans combined with visible provenance logic.
- `interface_graphic`: code/graphic-native hierarchy, sharp labels, grids, symbols, state clarity.
- `mixed_media`: exactly named layers with ownership, occlusion, interaction, and a shared palette/hierarchy.

## Mixing Rule

Good:

```text
primary aesthetic: luxury_editorial
secondary influence: tactile handcrafted paper construction
mix rule: editorial spacing and typography control hierarchy; paper craft controls only object construction and cast-shadow texture
```

Bad:

```text
cinematic + anime + oil painting + cyberpunk + watercolor + surreal
```

The primary family controls the global hierarchy. The secondary influence controls one named layer. Neither may silently change subject identity, story facts, literal text, or cultural context.

## Genre and Culture

Genre family and aesthetic family are separate. Horror can be cinematic naturalism, expressionist, archival, handcrafted, or graphic. Science fiction can be documentary, luxury editorial, brutal institutional, ecological, or playful animation.

For regional or cultural systems, describe material, craft, architecture, ritual use, color ownership, and historical context. Do not build a style from floating symbols or combine unrelated cultures as decoration.

## Quality Gate

- Medium behavior is visible and internally coherent.
- One primary aesthetic owns hierarchy.
- Palette, line/shape, texture/material, light, composition, and optics/rendering agree.
- Secondary influence has one clear job.
- Intentional grain, brushwork, seams, misregistration, or hand marks survive artifact cleanup.
- Forbidden drift names the most likely cheap or generic failure, not every style in history.
