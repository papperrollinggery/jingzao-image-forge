# Shot Tension, Exaggeration, and Spatial Dynamics

Use this reference for beautiful hero shots, battles, action, dance, fashion movement, giant scale, emotional pressure, extreme angles, deliberate distortion, or any request where foreground/midground/background relationships must create energy.

## Design Order

```text
viewer task -> dominant read -> tension source -> action vector and counterforce ->
foreground/midground/background roles -> camera position and lens projection ->
exaggeration budget -> distortion strategy -> motion evidence -> readability guard
```

“Beautiful” must resolve into visible mechanisms: silhouette, negative space, value/color hierarchy, material contrast, light direction, depth rhythm, action line, and a controlled resting area.

## Dominant and Secondary Reads

- The dominant read lands first at thumbnail size.
- The secondary read rewards closer inspection without competing.
- Beauty comes from hierarchy and relation, not equal detail everywhere.
- A shot may be visually rich, but only one relationship, contact, transformation, or product promise should own the peak.

## Tension and Action

- `action_vector`: where force, gaze, body, vehicle, cloth, or environment moves.
- `counterforce`: resistance, weight, opponent, ground reaction, drag, impact, or environmental pressure.
- Freeze one action phase: prepare, initiate, contact, response, or hold.
- Motion evidence may include cloth lag, hair follow-through, debris origin, water displacement, body compression, grip change, foot contact, and directional blur on the moving part only.
- A strong battle image shows geography and consequence, not only a heroic pose and particles.

## Depth Roles

- Foreground: proves viewer position, scale, speed, danger, concealment, or parallax.
- Midground: owns the primary subject/action/contact.
- Background: proves destination, consequence, world scale, threat, or escape route.
- Depth transition states how the eye moves between layers.
- Occlusion is useful when it creates scale, participation, discovery, or pressure; it must not hide the decisive information.

## Exaggeration Budgets

- `none`: objective, diagrammatic, catalog, documentary, or exact reconstruction.
- `restrained`: one subtle enhancement in angle, compression, light, or color.
- `strong`: one clearly designed variable plus preserved body/space readability.
- `extreme`: deliberate spectacle or graphic impact; requires a realism/spatial anchor and a readability guard.

Do not exaggerate camera, color, VFX, pose, light, and motion simultaneously unless the brief explicitly needs maximal abstraction.

## Distortion Strategies

- `optical`: barrel/pincushion, anamorphic edge behavior, focus breathing, spherical or vintage softness.
- `perspective`: near/far scale stretch, foreshortening, long-lens compression, low/high viewpoint.
- `spatial`: designed impossible relation, folded architecture, scale discontinuity, dream geography.
- `motion`: directional smear, rolling-shutter-like skew, radial movement, shutter-driven trails.
- `graphic`: line, shape, panel, typography, or 2D/3D layer distortion.

Every distortion needs a viewer effect and a protected anchor. Distortion that merely makes the image “dynamic” becomes random deformation.

## Camera Controls

- Height, distance, pitch, yaw, and roll define viewer position.
- Focal length and lens projection define perspective; distance must be considered with focal length.
- Camera roll is rare and motivated by imbalance, collision, subjectivity, or graphic force.
- Edge behavior states whether faces, limbs, architecture, or products may stretch near frame edges.
- Crop pressure states what may leave frame and why.
- Camera state distinguishes locked, handheld, tracking, orbiting, crane, drone, vehicle-mounted, or impossible graphic viewpoint.

## Gate

- Action and counterforce agree with body/material response.
- Foreground, midground, and background each have a job.
- Camera/lens/distortion serve the dominant read.
- Exaggeration has a declared budget.
- Motion evidence has a physical origin.
- The image retains one quiet or stable region so visual richness remains readable.
- Explicit user style, reference, capsule, color pipeline, and medium remain authoritative when the scenario changes.
